Lockstep harness

Two copies of room 36 are given the same seed and the same buttons, frame by frame, and their state is hashed after every step. The left copy is drawn every frame and the right one only now and then, so that anything drawing does to the game shows up as a difference. With a session (net) the two are joined by a wire that delays, reorders and drops. Serve the site over http to use this (frames from disk cannot be reached).
