Indoor Weather

2026. 0 cells of air, their pressure solved 0 times a second on your graphics card.

Move through it and the air moves with you, then keeps going. Every curl comes from the two equations that run the real weather, solved on a grid far too coarse to forecast anything and just fine enough to fool the eye.

Nothing in here was animated. There is no list of swirls to play back. The smoke goes where the arrows send it, the arrows push on each other, and what that ends up looking like is left entirely to the equations.
