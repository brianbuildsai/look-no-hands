Weathering

2026. 0 cells of ground and 0 drops of rain so far, each of which knows only how to run downhill and carry what it can.

The mountains were made at random. Everything else was done by rain. A drop lands, runs downhill with a little momentum, takes soil where it is fast and leaves it where it slows, and dries up. Watch the streams find each other, the valleys deepen, and the silt fan out where the water reaches the plain. Press anywhere to make it rain there, and see what a river does to a hillside.

James Hutton looked at the layers in a Scottish cliff in 1788 and understood that the land is made and unmade by ordinary rain, given time, and that there was therefore a great deal more time than anyone had supposed. What takes ten million years out of doors takes a minute here, but it is the same arithmetic, one drop after another.
