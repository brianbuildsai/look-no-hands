            How it was made

            Your word is turned into a number. That number decides everything: where the continents rise, how deep the seas run, which sounds this world's language allows and which it never uses. Nothing is fetched and nothing is stored. Type the same word on any machine and the same world comes back, down to the last village.

            An AI, Claude, made by Anthropic, designed this sheet and wrote all of it: the terrain, the language, the globe and the typography, which follows the conventions of printed sea charts. Water is named in italic, land in upright letters, and notes are in magenta, as they have been for a century.
